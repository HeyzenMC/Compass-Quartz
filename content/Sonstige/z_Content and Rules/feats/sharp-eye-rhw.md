# Sharp Eye
*Source: RHW*  

When you take the [Search](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/actions.md#Search) or [Study](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/actions.md#Study) action, you can give yourself [Advantage](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/variant-rules/advantage-xphb.md) on any ability check made as part of that action. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your [Proficiency Bonus](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/variant-rules/proficiency-xphb.md), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [Long Rest](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/variant-rules/long-rest-xphb.md). If the check fails, the use of this feature isn't expended.
