# Shield of Far Sight
*Armor ([shield](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/items/shield-xphb.md)), rare*  


- **Armor Class**: 2
- **Weight**: 6.0 lbs.

A [mind flayer](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/bestiary/aberration/mind-flayer-xmm.md) skilled at crafting magic items creates a shield of far sight by harvesting an eye from an intelligent humanoid and magically implanting it on the outer surface of a nonmagical shield. The shield becomes a magic item once the eyes is implanted, whereupon the [mind flayer](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/bestiary/aberration/mind-flayer-xmm.md) can give the shield to a thrall or hang it on a wall in its lair. As long as the shield is on the same plane of existence as its creator, the [mind flayer](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/bestiary/aberration/mind-flayer-xmm.md) can see through the shield's eye, which has [darkvision](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/senses.md#Darkvision) out to a range of 60 feet. While peering through this magical eye, the [mind flayer](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/bestiary/aberration/mind-flayer-xmm.md) can use its Mind Blast action as though it were standing behind the shield.

If a shield of far sight is destroyed, the [mind flayer](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/bestiary/aberration/mind-flayer-xmm.md) that created it is [blinded](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/conditions.md#Blinded) for `2d12` hours.

*Source: Volo's Guide to Monsters p. 81*
