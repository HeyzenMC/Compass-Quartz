# Spirit Medium
*Source: RHW*  
![](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/backgrounds/img/spirit-medium.webp#right)

- **Ability Scores.** Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom  
- **Feat.** A Dark Gift feat of your choice ([Gathered Whispers](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/feats/gathered-whispers-rhw.md) is recommended)  
- **Skill Proficiencies.** [Insight](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/skills.md#Insight) and [Religion](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/skills.md#Religion)  
- **Tool Proficiencies.** Choose one kind of [Gaming Set](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/items/gaming-set-xphb.md)  
- **Equipment.** Choose A or B: (A) [Dagger](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/items/dagger-xphb.md), [Gaming Set](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/items/gaming-set-xphb.md) (same as above), [Basket](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/items/basket-xphb.md), [Bell](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/items/bell-xphb.md), 8 Candles, [Ink](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/items/ink-xphb.md), [Ink Pen](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/items/ink-pen-xphb.md), [Paper](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/items/paper-xphb.md) (5 sheets), [Tinderbox](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/items/tinderbox-xphb.md), [Traveler's Clothes](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/items/travelers-clothes-xphb.md), and 32 GP; or (B) 50 GP  

Through strange rituals and fateful encounters, you've discovered that you bear a unique connection to the spirits of the dead and damned. Your body is a conduit for such spirits, granting you prescient insight from beyond. But these spirits' intentions aren't always innocent, and their gifts come at a haunting cost.
