# Lightning Generator
*Level 13 Bastion facility*  

- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Space**: Roomy (16 sq)
- **Hirelings**: 1
- **Order**: empower

A Lightning Generator can transform atmospheric electricity to serve a variety of useful functions.

## Charm of Electromancy

After spending a Long Rest in your Lightning Generator, you take `8d6` Lightning damage and you gain a magical [Charm](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/rewards/charm-of-electromancy-rhw.md) that lasts for 7 days (see the "Dungeon Master's Guide" for rules on "Charms").

## Revivification

When you cast the spell [Raise Dead](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/spells/raise-dead-xphb.md) and [Resurrection](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/spells/resurrection-xphb.md) while in this facility, the cost of that spell's Material components are reduced by 1,000 GP (minimum of 0).

## Empower: Lightning Life

The facility's hireling channels the Generator's energy to cast [Animate Objects](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/spells/animate-objects-xphb.md), targeting one Large or Huge object of your choice in the Bastion. The spell's duration is 7 days and doesn't require [Concentration](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/conditions.md#Concentration).
