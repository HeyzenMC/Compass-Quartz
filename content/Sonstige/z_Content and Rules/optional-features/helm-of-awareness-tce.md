# Helm of Awareness
*Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything p. 21*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 10, A helmet (requires attunement)

While wearing this helmet, a creature has advantage on initiative rolls. In addition, the wearer can't be [surprised](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/conditions.md#Surprised), provided it isn't [incapacitated](Sonstige/z_Content%20and%20Rules/conditions.md#Incapacitated).
