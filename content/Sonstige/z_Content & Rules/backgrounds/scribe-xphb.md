# Scribe
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 184*  
![](Sonstige/z_Content%20&%20Rules/backgrounds/img/scribe.webp#right)

- **Ability Scores.** Dexterity, Intelligence, Wisdom  
- **Feat.** [Skilled](Sonstige/z_Content%20&%20Rules/feats/skilled-xphb.md)  
- **Skill Proficiencies.** [Investigation](Sonstige/z_Content%20&%20Rules/skills.md#Investigation), [Perception](Sonstige/z_Content%20&%20Rules/skills.md#Perception)  
- **Tool Proficiency.** [Calligrapher's Supplies](Sonstige/z_Content%20&%20Rules/items/calligraphers-supplies-xphb.md)  
- **Equipment.** Choose A or B: (A) [Calligrapher's Supplies](Sonstige/z_Content%20&%20Rules/items/calligraphers-supplies-xphb.md), [Fine Clothes](Sonstige/z_Content%20&%20Rules/items/fine-clothes-xphb.md), [Lamp](Sonstige/z_Content%20&%20Rules/items/lamp-xphb.md), [Oil](Sonstige/z_Content%20&%20Rules/items/oil-xphb.md) (3 flasks), [Parchment](Sonstige/z_Content%20&%20Rules/items/parchment-xphb.md) (12 sheets), 23 GP; or (B) 50 GP  

You spent formative years in a scriptorium, a monastery dedicated to the preservation of knowledge, or a government agency, where you learned to write with a clear hand and produce finely written texts. Perhaps you scribed government documents or copied tomes of literature. You might have some skill as a writer of poetry, narrative, or scholarly research. Above all, you have a careful attention to detail, helping you avoid introducing mistakes to the documents you copy and create.
