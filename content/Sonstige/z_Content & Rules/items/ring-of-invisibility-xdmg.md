# Ring of Invisibility
*Ring, legendary (requires attunement)*  
![](Sonstige/z_Content%20&%20Rules/items/img/ring-of-invisibility.webp#right)


While wearing this ring, you can take a [Magic](Sonstige/z_Content%20&%20Rules/actions.md#Magic) action to give yourself the [Invisible](Sonstige/z_Content%20&%20Rules/conditions.md#Invisible) condition. You remain [Invisible](Sonstige/z_Content%20&%20Rules/conditions.md#Invisible) until the ring is removed or until you take a [Bonus Action](Sonstige/z_Content%20&%20Rules/variant-rules/bonus-action-xphb.md) to become visible again.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 293. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
