# Brooch of Shielding
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*  
![](Sonstige/z_Content%20&%20Rules/items/img/brooch-of-shielding.webp#right)


While wearing this brooch, you have [Resistance](Sonstige/z_Content%20&%20Rules/variant-rules/resistance-xphb.md) to Force damage, and you have [Immunity](Sonstige/z_Content%20&%20Rules/variant-rules/immunity-xphb.md) to damage from the [Magic Missile](Sonstige/z_Content%20&%20Rules/spells/magic-missile-xphb.md) spell.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 241. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
