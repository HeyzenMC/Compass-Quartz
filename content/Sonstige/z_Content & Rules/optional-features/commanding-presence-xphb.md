# Commanding Presence
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 95*  

When you make a Charisma ([Intimidation](Sonstige/z_Content%20&%20Rules/skills.md#Intimidation), [Performance](Sonstige/z_Content%20&%20Rules/skills.md#Performance), or [Persuasion](Sonstige/z_Content%20&%20Rules/skills.md#Persuasion)) check, you can expend one Superiority Die and add that die to the roll.
